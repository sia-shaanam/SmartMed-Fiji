Prescriptions for Fever

Take paracetamol 500mg every 6 hours. Drink plenty of fluids and rest.

Prescriptions for Cough

Use a cough syrup like Benadryl 2 teaspoons, 3 times a day. Stay hydrated.

Prescriptions for Heart Attack

Aspirin 325mg immediately, followed by urgent medical attention.

Prescriptions for Flu

Take antiviral medication like Tamiflu. Rest and drink plenty of fluids.

Prescriptions for Asthma

Inhaler (Albuterol) 2 puffs every 4-6 hours as needed. Use a preventive inhaler if prescribed.
